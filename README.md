A short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty "The poem about stopping"](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-stopping-20261121-1927).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic, no network)
writes the poem to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).

The poem — *Still* (179 characters), picked from 7 candidates scored against
the judge's five criteria (see `research/poem-about-stopping-candidates.md`
in the workspace):

> I stopped in the middle of the sentence
> you never finished.
> I did not fill it.
> I did not leave.
> I stayed where the words ran out,
> and the silence
> was the first thing
> I ever chose.

python3 main.py
